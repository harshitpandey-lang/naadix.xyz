Welcome to Naadix

Naadix is a Robotics and STEM education institute where students learn Robotics, Coding, Artificial Intelligence, and Foundation subjects in a practical and engaging way. Our programs combine Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics concepts with real applications so students understand both theory and implementation.

From guided beginner modules to advanced project work, students build with electronics, sensors, controllers, and coding tools to create meaningful prototypes. This project-based approach develops confidence, conceptual clarity, and strong technical fundamentals from an early stage.

Unlike traditional coaching, Naadix focuses on hands-on learning, innovation, and problem solving. We expose students early to modern technology and help them think like creators by turning ideas into working models and real-world solutions.

Hands-on Robotics

Students build real robots and learn electronics, sensors, and programming through guided practical projects.

Innovation Mindset

We help students turn their ideas into working prototypes while developing creativity, logical thinking, and problem solving.

Courses We Provide

Age-appropriate STEM pathways designed to build technical skills, conceptual understanding, and innovation through practical learning.

Robotics (Class 5-6)

Introduction to Robotics, Micro:bit based projects, basic electronics, simple sensors, block-based coding, and STEM fundamentals through experiments.

Robotics (Class 7-8)

Arduino basics, sensors and motors, robotics projects, programming fundamentals, line follower robot, and obstacle avoiding robot.

Robotics (Class 9-10)

ESP32 and Arduino advanced projects, IoT basics, embedded systems introduction, robotics automation, and real prototype building.

AI and Coding

Python basics, AI fundamentals, logic building, simple machine learning concepts, and problem solving for school students.

Foundation Courses

Classes 5-10 for Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with focus on conceptual clarity, strong fundamentals, and exam preparation.

Passion Counselling & Idea Development

Helping students discover their passion, develop innovative ideas, and convert those ideas into working prototypes.
